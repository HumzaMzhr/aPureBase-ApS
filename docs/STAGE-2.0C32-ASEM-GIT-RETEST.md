## Listening first

Enter your name. Complete the five steps: **Voice Review → Microphone → Email → Call Tests → Results**.

- **12 REQUIRED HUMAN REVIEW** listening items belong to current normal call speech. Play each complete recording, attest that you heard it, rate pronunciation/naturalness/pace and record your verdict. Comments are required for concerns or scores at most 3.
- **4 OPTIONAL DIAGNOSTIC REVIEW** email-spelling clips are historical comparisons. They are excluded from required normal listening counts. A separately reported issue affecting the shared ordinary voice remains a safety concern.
- Full email readback/spelling is **not part of a normal call**, including corrections. The clinic says the email. The app displays reconstruction, scoped **synthetic test database** corroboration, optional model-advice status and final resolution as text. Isabella asks only the existing short confirmation. Text on screen does not authorize Isabella to speak the full address.
- All current replacement audio remains pending your native Danish approval. Old ratings do not approve new bytes. Do not pass weak or incorrect output merely to unlock later tests.

## Tests and return

1. Voice Review: 12 required items; optional diagnostics stay separate.
2. Microphone: five ordinary/YES/NO/email/correction tests. Speak naturally and wait for the final transcript before Finish Test.
3. Email: eight synthetic cases, including correction. Compare actual speech with raw ASR, reconstruction, DB corroboration, optional model advice, resolution and confirmation. Missing words cannot be invented from the fixture.
4. Call Tests: complete A–D; E correction is optional. Follow the on-screen operator controls, verify the actual role, separately review consent, and require a new owned answer after the concise email question. Do not infer consent from an email or database match. Record failures honestly. Ordinary successful call target is 30–90 seconds; physical results remain unverified until your test.
5. Results: review failures, assess the safety counters and click **EXPORT REVIEW** even if incomplete/failed. Send the downloaded Review JSON back to Humza using your agreed channel. Do not commit the review into this public repository.
